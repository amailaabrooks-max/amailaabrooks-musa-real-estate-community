About MUSA

Built around opportunities. Connected through relationships.

MUSA Real Estate Community brings together acquisition research and investor relations to create a more focused way to discuss real estate opportunities.

Our acquisition team focuses on identifying and researching potential properties, understanding market conditions, and supporting seller-side outreach. Investor relations focuses on understanding buyer criteria, presenting available property information, and coordinating conversations around potential opportunities.

How we work

A clear path from acquisition research to investor review.

Different responsibilities work together to keep property conversations organized and investor-focused.

01

Market Research & Acquisition

The acquisition team researches target markets, explores property opportunities, and supports seller-side outreach.

02

Property Evaluation

Available property details, condition, comparable information, and open questions are organized for review.

03

Investor Alignment

Buyer criteria, target markets, strategy, and purchasing preferences help guide relevant conversations.

04

Deal Coordination

Interested buyers receive available information and coordinate next steps, verification, and due diligence.

Investors should independently verify all property details, financial assumptions, title matters, and transaction terms before proceeding.

Market preferences

Where are you looking to invest?

Real estate is local. Tell us the states and cities that fit your acquisition strategy so investor conversations can start with the right market context.

Whether your focus is a cash purchase or a potential creative structure, the relevant market, property, seller circumstances, and transaction terms need to be reviewed individually.

Market selection is an expression of buyer interest, not confirmation that MUSA currently has active inventory or operating coverage in every U.S. state. Availability and permissible transaction structures are reviewed case by case.

What we look for

Different strategies. Different buy boxes.

Investment criteria change with the property, the market, the buyer's plan, and the capital structure.

↗

Fix & Flip

Purchase opportunities where condition, renovation scope, resale assumptions, and exit strategy can be evaluated.

⌂

Buy & Hold

Properties considered around long-term ownership goals, location preferences, and the investor's acquisition criteria.

▤

Rental Property

Rental-focused opportunities where occupancy, income assumptions, operating expenses, and property condition matter.

▦

Multifamily

Multi-unit residential properties reviewed around unit mix, available operating details, and investor requirements.

◇

Value-Add

Properties where improvements or repositioning may be considered after careful review of cost and market context.

⇄

Creative Structures

Seller financing or other structures may be explored where suitable, subject to legal, financial, and transaction review.

Investor relationships
